# App wordmarks

Original Netflix and Apple TV wordmarks are included as Android vectors, with aspect ratios preserved. White or brand/dark tint is applied to match pill focus.

- Netflix Inc.: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Netflix_2015_logo.svg (source: netflix.com; PD-textlogo).
- Apple Inc.: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apple_TV_logo.svg (source: apple.com; PD-textlogo).

The marks identify installed apps. They remain trademarks of their owners.

- Prime Video (2024), YouTube light logo (2017), and YouTube Music full logo: original brand wordmarks via Wikimedia Commons (PD-textlogo), converted to monochrome Android vectors.
- JioHotstar: original mark from https://seeklogo.com/vector-logo/613744/jiohotstar; rendered from its source symbol and wordmark without substituting text.
- Category ocean photograph: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1475924156734-496f6cac6ec1 (Unsplash License: https://unsplash.com/license). This is a photographic fallback, not the exact photograph in the camera references.

- Spotify, Sony and YouTube Music symbol outlines: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons (CC0).
- Sony LIV transparent LIV lettering: https://www.sonyliv.com/assets/images/sony-liv.png.
- Airtel Xstream: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Airtel_Xstream_logo.svg (PD-textlogo).
- F1 TV: https://logodownload.org/f1-tv-logo/ (brand identification wordmark, transparent vector).

- ZEE5 (2025): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ZEE5_2025.svg (PD-textlogo); original vector geometry and color gradient.

- Movie/TV original backdrops and title logos: TMDB (https://www.themoviedb.org/), retrieved by title ID. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Viewing offers are event-specific source data, not a guarantee of subscription entitlement or TV deep-link support.
